# HINDPETRO — Hindustan Petroleum Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. HPCL is a government-owned oil refining and marketing company (ONGC subsidiary), operating two refineries (Mumbai & Visakhapatnam) with 18 MMTPA combined capacity and India's 2nd largest retail fuel network of 20,000+ petrol pumps.
2. Its moat is a regulatory-protected marketing margin on auto fuels (petrol/diesel), 50%+ government ownership ensuring stable operations, and irreplaceable logistics infrastructure (pipelines, terminals, depots) across western and southern India.
3. Management is government-appointed (competent but constrained), with the key trust factor being the government's historical reluctance to fully deregulate margins — this creates both a floor and ceiling for profitability.
4. At ~4.7x PE with 31% ROE and 6%+ dividend yield, the valuation reflects the market's fear of windfall profit taxes and government intervention in fuel pricing, not the structural earnings power from India's growing fuel demand.
5. Downside is cushioned by regulated marketing margins (government will not let OMCs bleed), strategic asset base (replacement cost > market cap), and 6%+ dividend yield providing a return floor even without price appreciation.
