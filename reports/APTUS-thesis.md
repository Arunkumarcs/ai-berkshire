# APTUS — Aptus Value Housing Finance Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Aptus is a specialized housing finance company (HFC) serving low- and middle-income self-employed borrowers in tier-2/3 towns of South India, providing home loans, LAP, and small business loans at competitive rates.
2. Its moat is deep underwriting expertise in the informal income segment (no bank-served), hyper-local branch presence in 300+ unbanked towns, and NIM of ~12% that reflects pricing power from specialized credit assessment.
3. Management (promoter P. Balakrishna ~23% stake) is trustworthy, with zero NPAs historically, conservative leverage (D/E 1.56 for an HFC is moderate), and consistent 20%+ ROE since IPO.
4. At ~15.1x PE with 25% earnings growth and PEG ~0.65, the market prices in risk from economic slowdown in its borrower segment, but ignores AUM headroom as housing penetration in its states is still <50% of national average.
5. Downside is cushioned by strong asset quality (GNPA <1%), a granular loan book (avg ticket ~₹10L), 90%+ secured lending, and promoter skin in the game; worst case is a credit cycle that temporarily drops ROE to 15%.
