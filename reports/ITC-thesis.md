# ITC — ITC Limited Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. ITC is a diversified conglomerate generating ~80% of profits from cigarettes (India monopoly), with growing FMCG (foods, personal care), hotels, paperboards, and agri-business divisions.
2. Its moat is a regulatory-created cigarette duopoly (with Godfrey Phillips), unmatched distribution network of ~6M retail touchpoints, and brand equity of 80+ year old trademarks like Gold Flake, Classic, and Navy Cut.
3. Management is trustworthy, maintaining 29%+ ROE and 300x interest coverage, with a conservative balance sheet (D/E 0.03) and consistent dividend payout of ~80%+ over 5 years.
4. At ~17x PE (vs 10-year median ~24x), the stock prices in regulatory tax hike fears but not the structural volume recovery from GST stabilization and the optional value of FMCG/hotels turnaround.
5. Downside is cushioned by 5%+ dividend yield, Rs 40K+ Cr cash on books, and the pricing power to pass tax increases to consumers that historically restrains volume decline to 2-4% per annum.
