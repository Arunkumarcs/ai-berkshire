# JKTYRE — JK Tyre & Industries Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. JK Tyre is one of India's top 3 tyre manufacturers, making tyres for trucks/buses (TBR leader), passenger cars, LCVs, farm, and OTR vehicles, with 11 manufacturing plants across India and Mexico.
2. Its moat is technological leadership as India's pioneer of radial truck tyres, OE relationships with every major Indian auto OEM, and a deep branded replacement network (6,000+ dealers, 900+ exclusive brand shops).
3. Management (Singhania family promoter ~52%, in control since 1951) is trustworthy with a long track record of navigating rubber cycles, maintaining market share, and recently delivering 57% net profit growth in FY26.
4. At ~13.4-15.2x PE well below sector average of 26x, the market prices in raw material volatility (rubber/crude) and Mexico business risks, ignoring the structural TBR radialization tailwind and 11-13% price hikes announced for H1FY27.
5. Downside is limited by diversified global manufacturing footprint (India + Mexico), moderate D/E of 0.81, and replacement demand (~65% of revenue) that is sticky and non-discretionary for commercial vehicles.
