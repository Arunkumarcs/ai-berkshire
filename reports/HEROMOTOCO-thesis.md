# HEROMOTOCO — Hero MotoCorp Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Hero MotoCorp is the world's largest two-wheeler manufacturer by volume (~8M units/year), producing affordable commuter motorcycles and scooters sold through the largest dealership network in India (6,000+ outlets).
2. Its moat is unmatched distribution reach in rural and semi-urban India, a 3:1 brand recall advantage over rivals in the commuter segment (Splendor/Passion), and cost-efficient manufacturing at scale with 9.5M unit annual capacity.
3. Management (Pawan Munjal-led, family promoter ~35%) is trustworthy, evidenced by 28%+ ROE, 50%+ dividend payout consistently, near zero debt, and market share defense despite aggressive competition from Honda and Bajaj.
4. At ~17.2x PE near 5-year lows, the market prices in cyclical rural demand weakness and EV disruption fears, ignoring Hero's dominant position and eventual EV transition via Vida brand.
5. Downside is limited by net cash balance sheet (~₹12,000 Cr), 3.7% dividend yield, and irreplaceable dealership network moat in rural India that persists even in EV era.
