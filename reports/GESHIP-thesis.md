# GESHIP — Great Eastern Shipping Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Great Eastern Shipping is India's largest private-sector shipping company owning a diversified fleet of crude/product tankers, LPG carriers, and dry bulk carriers serving global energy and commodity trade routes.
2. Its moat is a 75-year operating track record, strong relationships with national oil companies, a modern fuel-efficient fleet, and conservative fleet management with low leverage (D/E 0.06).
3. Management (Sheth family promoting ~25%) is trustworthy with impeccable capital allocation — net cash positive, AAA-rated (CARE), buying ships counter-cyclically, and maintaining strong dividends (₹11.70/sh interim).
4. At ~6.9x PE with 19% ROE and EV/EBITDA ~4.7x, the valuation severely discounts shipping cycle normalization fears, ignoring the structural shift in tanker demand from geopolitical supply rerouting.
5. Downside is limited by net cash balance sheet (cash > debt), fleet at conservative book values, and the company's ability to cut dividends only (not face solvency risk) in a downturn; worst-case PE floor ~5x.
