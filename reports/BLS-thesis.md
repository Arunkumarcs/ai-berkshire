# BLS — BLS International Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. BLS International provides outsourced visa, passport, and consular services to diplomatic missions worldwide, processing millions of applications annually through its network of over 60,000 staff across 45+ countries.
2. Its moat is long-term government contracts (typically 5-10 years), high entry barriers from security clearances and biometric infrastructure investment, and cost advantages from scale that makes in-sourcing uneconomical for most missions.
3. Management (Sh. Shabir, promoter ~28%) is trustworthy, demonstrated by 37% revenue CAGR, 30%+ ROE, nearly debt-free balance sheet, and consistent dividend payments since 2022.
4. At ~14.3x PE on 35%+ earnings growth, the stock is undervalued relative to tech-enabled service peers; the multiple compression from 52-week high of ₹415 reflects overhang from geopolitical tensions, not underlying business momentum.
5. Downside is protected by government contract lock-ins (revenue visibility 3-5 years), asset-light model (no inventory risk), and ₹2,500 Cr expansion plan signaling management confidence in tender pipeline.
