## 2. Business Nature

**One-sentence**: India's largest private-sector shipping company, owning and operating a fleet of 41 vessels that transport crude oil, petroleum products, LPG, and dry bulk commodities globally.

**Revenue Structure (FY2026)**:
| Segment | Contribution | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Crude Tankers | ~35% | 5 crude carriers. Spot market exposed. |
| Product Tankers | ~30% | 17 product carriers. Transport refined fuels. |
| Dry Bulk | ~25% | 14 carriers (capesize, kamsarmax, supramax). |
| LPG | ~5% | 4 LPG carriers. |
| Offshore (Greatship) | ~5% | Oilfield services. Long-term contracts. |

**Business Model**: Asset-heavy, cyclical. ~60-70% spot market exposure. High operating leverage — when rates are good, margins are spectacular (85%+ EBITDA margin). When rates are bad, the company still generates cash but at lower levels. Capital allocation is about buying/selling ships at the right point in the cycle.

**DYP追问**: What does this business actually do that creates value? It moves commodities across oceans efficiently. The value creation is timing the ship acquisition/sale cycle correctly. At the operating level, it's a commodity — ships are interchangeable, clients book the cheapest available vessel.
