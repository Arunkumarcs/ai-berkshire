# ICICIBANK — ICICI Bank Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. ICICI Bank is India's largest private sector bank by consolidated assets (~₹26L Cr), providing retail banking, corporate banking, SME lending, insurance (ICI Prudential, Lombard), asset management, and treasury services through 6,500+ branches.
2. Its moat is a diversified low-cost deposit franchise (CASA ~45%), robust technology platform with 17,000+ ATMs and digital leadership, and cross-selling synergy from its insurance and asset management subsidiaries.
3. Management under Sandeep Bakhshi (CEO) is highly trustworthy, demonstrated by dramatic asset quality improvement (GNPA from 8.5% in 2018 to ~2.0% now), 18%+ ROE sustaining, and conservative provisioning building buffers.
4. At ~18.8x PE in line with 5-year median, the valuation is fair for a top-tier private bank; the premium over HDFC Bank is narrow, reflecting ICICI's superior loan growth and margin expansion trajectory.
5. Downside is protected by strong provision coverage (PCR >75%), diversified loan book (retail 60%+), and systemic importance — worst case is a macro shock that drops ROE to 12-13% with a corresponding PE de-rating to ~15x.
