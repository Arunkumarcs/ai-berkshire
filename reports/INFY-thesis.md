# INFY — Infosys Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Infosys is an IT services & consulting business generating revenue by providing digital transformation, software development, and outsourcing services to global enterprises across 50+ countries.
2. Its moat is scale-driven cost advantage (328K employees, global delivery network), client switching costs from deep ERP/mainframe integration, and a trusted brand built over 4 decades.
3. Salil Parekh-led management is trustworthy, evidenced by consistent 30%+ ROE, disciplined capital allocation (buybacks + dividends), and conservative accounting with 4.5% cash yield.
4. At ~14.5x PE (vs 10-year median ~22x), the price discounts a cyclical IT spending slowdown, offering margin of safety if growth merely normalizes to 8-10% from recent 5% trough.
5. Downside is limited by net cash position (~₹400B+), dividend yield ~4.5%, and essential nature of IT spend — clients do not terminate Infosys contracts easily; the floor is replacement cost of the talent base.
