# M&M — Mahindra & Mahindra Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. M&M is India's leading SUV manufacturer (20%+ market share) and the world's largest tractor maker by volume, selling vehicles, farm equipment, and financial services across 100+ countries.
2. Its moat is dominant brand recall in Indian SUV segment (Bolero, Scorpio, XUV series), unmatched rural distribution network for tractors, and 7-decade customer trust in "Mahindra" brand synonymous with durability.
3. Management under CEO Anish Shah is trustworthy, demonstrated by 32%+ YoY profit growth in FY26, consistent 18%+ ROE, and disciplined capital allocation with focus on core auto + farm businesses.
4. At ~20.6x PE, the valuation is fair but justified by 25% revenue growth, industry-leading SUV cycle, and tractor upcycle from normal monsoons and government farm spending.
5. Downside is mitigated by strong balance sheet (D/E 0.34 excluding financial services), diversified earnings base (finance + farm + auto), and structural under-penetration of Indian auto market.
