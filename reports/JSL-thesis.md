# JSL — Jindal Stainless Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Jindal Stainless is India's largest and the world's 4th largest stainless steel producer, with 2.9 MTPA capacity across hot-rolled, cold-rolled, and value-added products serving auto, construction, railway, and consumer goods industries.
2. Its moat is first-mover advantage in Indian stainless steel with 50%+ domestic market share, backward integration through ferro alloys, and government protection via anti-dumping duties that keep cheap Chinese imports at bay.
3. Management (Jindal family, promoter ~57%) is competent but governance history warrants caution — the CFO transition in June 2026 needs monitoring; however, the company has delivered 349% profit CAGR over 5 years with 17.6% ROE.
4. At ~18.5x PE near historical median, the stock is fairly valued for a cyclical steel company; the premium over commodity peers is justified by industry leadership, but upside depends on stainless penetration increasing from 3.5% to 5% of total Indian steel.
5. Downside is the cyclical steel earnings risk — if global stainless prices normalize, JSL's profits could halve, implying a worst-case PE of ~30x on depressed earnings; however, low D/E (0.38) and domestic demand insulation from China devaluation provides a floor.
