## Sector Summaries

### OMCs (BPCL, HINDPETRO, CHENNPETRO)
All PSU oil companies with regulated marketing margins. HINDPETRO/BPCL pass on PE <5 + div yields >5%, but CHENNPETRO fails on smaller scale. Buffett rule: regulated businesses are OK if you buy cheap enough. OMCs are cheap enough → PASS for BPCL/HINDPETRO.

### Banks (ICICIBANK, IDFCFIRSTB, FEDERALBNK, KARURVYSYA, SOUTHBANK, TMB, INDUSINDBK)
ICICI is the only clear PASS. IndusInd/Federal are borderline. IDFCFIRSTB fails on ROE. Small regional banks (Karur, South, TMB) fail on size and moat.

### IT (INFY, TECHM, ECLERX, ZENSARTECH)
INFY is a PASS. TECHM/ZENSARTECH/ECLERX are weaker peers in a commoditizing sector. INFY's ROE 32%, PE 14.4, 4.5% yield is a Buffett-style bargain.

### Pharma (NATCOPHARM, LUPIN, DRREDDY, ZYDUSLIFE, CIPLA, BIOCON)
LUPIN and ZYDUSLIFE pass. DRREDDY borders on ROE weakness. NATCOPHARM borders on lumpy earnings. BIOCON fails. Sector-wide: high R&D risk, regulatory dependency.

### Steel (JSL, JSWSTEEL, TATASTEEL)
All fail or border. Commodity cyclical at peak earnings. ROE looks artificially high at cycle top. TATASTEEL and JSWSTEEL fail on low ROE through cycle. JSL borders.

### Auto (M&M, HEROMOTOCO, BAJAJ-AUTO)
HEROMOTOCO passes (PE 16.8, debt-free, 3.8% yield). M&M and BAJAJ-AUTO border on valuation.

### FMCG/Consumer (ITC, KALYANKJIL, JYOTHYLAB, STOVEKRAFT)
ITC is the only clear PASS. Excellent ROE 29%, PE 16.8, 5.2% yield with cigarette moat. Others are unproven or too expensive.
