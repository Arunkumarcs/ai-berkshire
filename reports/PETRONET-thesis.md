# PETRONET — Petronet LNG Investment Thesis

**Established:** 2026-07-13
**Data Cutoff:** 2026-07-13

## Core Thesis

1. Petronet LNG operates India's largest LNG regasification terminals (Dahej 17.5 MMTPA, Kochi 5 MMTPA), importing ~33% of India's natural gas supply and processing ~75% of the country's LNG imports in a tolling-based business model.
2. Its moat is irreplaceable LNG import infrastructure with long-term take-or-pay contracts (GAIL, IOC, BPCL, ONGC as anchor customers and shareholders), regulatory monopoly at its terminal locations, and huge entry barriers to build competing terminals.
3. Management (Akshay Kumar Singh as MD & CEO) is competent and government-aligned (PROMOTER: GAIL + ONGC + IOC + BPCL each 12.5% = 50%), with a trustworthy track record of stable dividend payouts, 18.6% ROE, and conservative leverage management.
4. At ~10.6x PE below 10-year average of 12.4x, the stock prices in the near-term gas demand dip from high spot LNG prices, but ignores the structural story of India's gas share rising from 6% to 15% of energy mix per government policy.
5. Downside is cushioned by crown jewel—Dahej terminal is a natural monopoly with high utilization (~100%) & take-or-pay contracts providing predictable cash flows; net cash position, 3.7% dividend yield, and promoter ownership providing a floor.
